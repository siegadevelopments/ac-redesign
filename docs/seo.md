# Apprising Creatives — SEO & Search Visibility Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and AI-search visibility strategy for Apprising Creatives.

The goal is not to manipulate search rankings.

The goal is to make Apprising Creatives:

- Easy for search engines to understand
- Easy for users to discover
- Easy for AI search systems to interpret
- Credible for business decision-makers
- Relevant to high-intent prospects
- Technically accessible and performant

SEO decisions must support the company's business positioning and conversion goals.

---

# 10. Title Tag Guidelines

Title tags should:

- Clearly describe the page
- Include the primary topic naturally
- Be compelling to the target audience
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Usually place the company name toward the end

Example homepage:

> AI Software & Business Automation | Apprising Creatives

Alternative:

> Custom Software, AI & Business Automation | Apprising Creatives

Do not automatically append the company name if doing so makes the title awkward.

---

# 11. Meta Description Guidelines

Meta descriptions should:

- Explain the page clearly
- Match the page content
- Communicate value
- Encourage qualified clicks
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Avoid keyword stuffing

Example:

> Apprising Creatives builds custom software, AI integrations and business automation systems for growing businesses and technology agencies.

Meta descriptions should be written for humans first.

---

# 12. Heading Structure

Use one primary H1 per page unless there is a strong semantic reason otherwise.

Example:

H1:

H2:
> Technology built around your business.

H3:
> Custom Software

H3:
> AI Integration

H3:
> Business Automation

H3:
> Managed Application Support

Headings should communicate the information hierarchy.

Do not use headings simply to achieve visual styling.

# 13. URL Structure

Do not change URLs unnecessarily after publication.

Do not claim:

- Australian office
- Australian headquarters
- Australian address
- Australian team members

unless these are factually true.

Geographic targeting should be based on actual service availability and market focus.

/software-development-melbourne/

/software-development-brisbane/

Use structured data where it accurately describes the page.

Potential schema types:

- Organization
- WebSite

- Service
- Article

Do not add structured data simply for the sake of adding schema.

# 20. FAQ Content

FAQs should answer genuine customer questions.

Examples:

### Do I need custom software?

Not necessarily. We first look at the business problem and determine whether existing software, integration, automation or custom development is the most practical solution.

### Can you integrate AI into an existing system?

Yes. AI can often be introduced into an existing workflow rather than requiring an entirely new platform.

### Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Apprising can provide engineering capacity behind agencies delivering software, AI, automation and integration projects.

FAQ content should be useful to humans first.

Do not create hundreds of artificial FAQ questions for SEO.

---

Recommended structure:

# 29. Image SEO

Images should:

- Have descriptive filenames
- Use meaningful alt text when appropriate
- Be compressed
- Use modern formats where practical
- Avoid unnecessary decorative images
- Avoid large unoptimized hero images

Example filename:

`business-workflow-automation.webp`

Avoid:

`IMG_48291.jpg`

Alt text should describe meaningful image content.

Do not use alt text as a keyword stuffing mechanism.

SEO and performance are connected.

The website should prioritize:

- Fast initial load
- Minimal JavaScript
- Optimized images
- Responsive images
- Proper font loading
- Minimal layout shift
- Efficient animations
- Server-side rendering where appropriate
- Static generation where appropriate

# 31. Technical SEO

The implementation should include:

- Valid HTML
- Semantic markup
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Canonical URLs
- XML sitemap
- Robots.txt
- Metadata
- Open Graph metadata
- Twitter/X metadata where useful
- Proper redirects
- Clean URLs
- Mobile responsiveness
- HTTPS
- Indexability controls

---

# 32. Sitemap

The sitemap should contain indexable, canonical pages.

Do not include:

- Duplicate pages
- Internal utility pages
- Noindex pages
- Development routes
- Parameter variations
- Unimportant temporary pages

Update the sitemap automatically where practical.

---

# 33. Robots.txt

Robots directives should be conservative.

Do not accidentally block:

- Important pages
- CSS
- JavaScript required for rendering
- Images
- Search engine crawlers

Do not use robots.txt as a substitute for proper noindex handling.

---

# 34. Canonical URLs

Every indexable page should have a clear canonical URL.

Canonical URLs should use the preferred:

- Protocol
- Domain
- Path
- Trailing slash convention

Avoid inconsistent URL versions.

---

# 35. Open Graph

Important pages should have useful social sharing metadata.

Recommended:

- og:title
- og:description
- og:image
- og:url
- og:type

The homepage should have a professional branded social preview.

# 43. SEO Prioritization

When deciding whether to create or optimize a page, use this priority:

1. Business value
2. Search intent
3. Audience relevance
4. Content quality
5. Conversion opportunity
6. Topical relevance
7. Technical SEO
8. Keyword opportunity

Do not create pages simply because a keyword has search volume.

---

# 44. SEO Decision Rule

When SEO conflicts with user experience, prioritize the user.

When SEO conflicts with conversion clarity, prioritize clear communication.

When keyword targeting conflicts with truthful positioning, prioritize truthful positioning.

When visual effects conflict with performance, prioritize performance.

---

# 45. Definition of Success

SEO success for Apprising Creatives means:

- Qualified Australian prospects discover the company
- Search engines clearly understand the company's services
- AI search systems can accurately describe Apprising
- Important commercial pages gain visibility
- Website visitors understand the offering quickly
- Organic traffic produces meaningful business conversations
- Search visibility grows around relevant business problems
- The company builds long-term topical authority

The ultimate metric is not:

> How many visitors did we get?

It is:

> Did the right businesses find Apprising and start a conversation?
