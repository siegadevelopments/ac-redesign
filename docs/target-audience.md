# Apprising Creatives — Target Audience

## 1. Primary Ideal Customer Profile

### Definition

Australian founder-led digital, AI, automation, and software agencies with approximately 2–30 people that can sell technical projects but occasionally lack the engineering capacity to deliver them.

---

# 2. Primary Buyer

Typical decision-makers:

- Founder
- CEO
- Managing Director
- Technical Director
- CTO
- Head of Delivery

The buyer is usually commercially responsible for delivering client projects.

---

# 3. Agency Problems

Potential agency clients may experience:

### Capacity Problems

- Too many projects
- Not enough developers
- Internal developers are fully booked
- Tight project deadlines
- Need temporary engineering capacity

### Capability Problems

The agency can sell the project but lacks expertise in:

- Backend development
- APIs
- AI implementation
- Database architecture
- Complex integrations
- Automation
- Cloud infrastructure
- Custom application development

### Delivery Problems

- Projects are becoming technically complex
- Existing developers are overloaded
- Client expectations are increasing
- The agency needs a reliable technical partner

---

# 4. Agency Buying Triggers

Potential triggers include:

- New client won
- Large project won
- Existing team overloaded
- Developer resigned
- Client requests AI implementation
- Client requests custom software
- Client requests API integration
- Client requests automation
- Agency wants to expand technical offerings
- Agency wants to accept projects outside its internal capabilities

---

# 5. Agency Objections

Potential objections:

### "Will you take my client?"

Response positioning:

Apprising supports the agency's client relationship rather than competing for it.

---

### "Will the quality be good?"

Positioning:

Engineering quality, documentation, testing, maintainability, and professional delivery are priorities.

---

### "Will communication be difficult?"

Positioning:

Clear technical communication, defined responsibilities, documentation, and structured delivery.

---

### "Are you just cheap offshore developers?"

Never compete primarily on price.

Apprising should position itself as:

> A professional engineering partner for agencies that need reliable technical delivery capacity.

---

# 6. Secondary Ideal Customer Profile

Growing Australian businesses and SMEs.

Typical characteristics:

- 10–200 employees
- Growing operations
- Increasing administrative workload
- Multiple disconnected tools
- Manual data entry
- Spreadsheet-heavy workflows
- Repetitive tasks
- Operational bottlenecks

---

# 7. Business Problems

Common problems:

- Staff manually copy data between systems
- Employees spend hours on repetitive tasks
- Important information is spread across multiple systems
- Customers have difficulty booking or accessing services
- Reporting requires manual effort
- Existing software does not match business workflows
- Business processes depend heavily on individual employees
- Management lacks visibility into operations

---

# 8. Business Buying Triggers

Strong signals include:

- Rapid growth
- Hiring more administrative staff
- Operational inefficiency
- New business location
- New product/service launch
- Multiple software systems being adopted
- Spreadsheet dependency
- Increasing customer volume
- Need for internal dashboards
- Need for automation
- Need for AI integration
- Existing software becoming limiting

---

# 9. Buyer Psychology

The buyer is generally not buying "technology."

They are buying:

- Less manual work
- Faster operations
- Better visibility
- Fewer mistakes
- Better customer experience
- More capacity
- Scalable processes
- Reduced operational friction
- Better use of employee time

Therefore messaging should focus on outcomes before technical implementation.

---

# 10. Messaging Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy:

1. Business problem
2. Business impact
3. Solution
4. Technology
5. Implementation

Example:

Weak:

> We build AI-powered applications using Python and FastAPI.

Better:

> We automate repetitive business workflows and connect the systems your team relies on.

Then explain:

> When the right solution requires custom software or AI, we build it using modern, maintainable technology.

---

# 11. Audience Language

Use language such as:

- Business systems
- Operational efficiency
- Automation
- Integration
- Custom software
- Engineering capacity
- Workflow
- Connected systems
- Business processes
- Technical delivery
- Reliable engineering
- Practical AI

Avoid excessive use of:

- Disruption
- Revolutionise
- Cutting-edge
- Game-changing
- Next-generation
- AI-powered everything
- Digital transformation
- Leverage AI
- Synergy

---

# 12. Conversion Strategy

## For Businesses

Primary message:

> We build the systems your business needs to operate better.

CTA:

> Book a Discovery Call

---

## For Agencies

Primary message:

> You sell the solution. We help build it.

CTA:

> Partner With Us

---

# 13. Website Audience Priority

Priority order:

1. Qualified agency prospects
2. Qualified business owners/operators
3. Technical partners
4. General visitors

The website should not be designed primarily for developers.

It should be understandable to a business decision-maker.

---

# 14. Qualification Signals

High-value prospects typically have:

- A real business problem
- Budget
- Clear operational pain
- Existing systems
- A project timeline
- Decision-making authority
- Need for ongoing support
- Need for custom engineering

Low-value prospects include:

- People looking for the cheapest developer
- One-off low-budget websites
- Projects with no defined business value
- Requests for unrealistic AI features
- People seeking free technical consulting
