# First-Client Go-To-Market Plan (Agencies First, 60 Days)

Created: 2026-10-30

## Goal

Win the first paid project or paid trial within 60 days, and reach 3 to 5 agency conversations a week by week 4. These are targets to learn against, not forecasts.

## Constraints and decisions

- **Capacity:** 10+ hours a week, zero budget. Free channels only.
- **Starting point:** no outreach done yet. One real client (Lifestyle Medicine Gateway, "LMG"), no testimonial.
- **Lead audience:** agencies for the first 60 days. SME inbound runs quietly alongside through the problem box. Light SME outreach starts only once agency conversations are flowing, in one test vertical.
- **Agency funnel stays separate** from the SME funnel: `/for-agencies` then **Partner With Us** (`mailto:`). See `docs/conversion-strategy.md` and `docs/offers.md`.
- **Claims rules apply to all outreach copy:** no invented clients, metrics, testimonials or certifications (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/trust-and-claims.md`). Be upfront about team size and location (`docs/australian-market.md`).

## Hard rule: the 1,000+ email list is off limits

The list came from LMG. It is LMG's client data, held by Apprising as a service provider.

- Do not email it, and do not load it into Kit or any other tool.
- Reasons: consent those people gave covers LMG, not Apprising (Spam Act); direct-marketing rules under the Privacy Act; and LMG is the only real client. You are about to ask it for approval and a quote, so misuse would cost the relationship and the case study.
- Keep the data only where LMG's service needs it.
- This is not legal advice. If a use of the list is ever proposed, get LMG's written agreement and a legal opinion first.

## Compliance checklist for every outreach message

Check these against ACMA's current guidance before the first send: [Avoid sending spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam).

- ACMA's guidance and enforcement notices say an address being published does not by itself mean consent. The exception for conspicuously published addresses is narrow. Read the conditions yourself before relying on it.
- Commercial messages need clear sender identification and a working unsubscribe. ACMA's guidance says unsubscribe requests must be honoured within 5 working days. The unsubscribe must keep working for at least 30 days after sending, and must not need a login or extra personal details.
- Prefer channels and wording that are lower risk: one-to-one, personal, relevant to the person's role, sent to a business address, with an easy opt-out line.
- Keep a simple suppression list. Anyone who says no is never contacted again.
- Treat LinkedIn messages and calls as having their own platform rules. Follow LinkedIn's limits and don't automate sending.

## Phase 2: Week 1 to 4, direct agency outreach (about 8 hours a week)

1. **Build the target list (60 to 100 agencies), from public sources only:** agency websites, LinkedIn, public directories. Target Australian agencies of roughly 2 to 30 people that sell AI, automation or software work and look thin on engineering.
2. **Record for each:** agency name, contact person (founder or technical director), one specific thing they sell, a reason to contact them now, date contacted, outcome.
3. **Send 10 to 15 personalised one-to-one messages a week.** Each names something specific about their work and offers delivery capacity. Include who you are, where you're based, and an easy way to say no.
4. **Offer one low-commitment first step:** a 20-minute call, or a small scoped paid trial. Do not lead with a retainer.
5. **Follow up once** after about a week. If there's no reply, stop.
6. **Weekly quota, not endless research:** cap list-building at about 2 hours a week so outreach actually goes out.

## Phase 3: Week 2 onward, warm channels (about 2 hours a week)

- Tell your personal network what you do and which kind of introduction you want (an agency founder, or an SME owner with a messy process). Make the ask specific so it's easy to forward.
- Post on LinkedIn 1 or 2 times a week about real, verified work only. No invented results or testimonials.

## Phase 4: Week 4 to 8, review and adjust

- Weekly, count: messages sent, replies, calls booked, proposals sent, paid trials started.
- Keep the message, list segment and channel that got replies. Drop the rest.
- If agency conversations are flowing, add light SME outreach in one test vertical (from healthcare, logistics, tourism and hospitality, education, professional services). If they aren't, fix the agency message first.

## Risks and contingencies

- **Proof gap:** one client and no testimonial slows cold outreach. If LMG declines, lead with the work you can show from its public site and be honest about it. Don't inflate.
- **Reply rate stays low:** change one thing at a time (subject and opening line, target segment, offer) rather than everything at once.
- **Time creep:** list-building and personalisation expand to fill the week. Hold the 2-hour cap on research.
- **Overseas positioning:** state team size and location plainly in the first message. It builds trust and removes a later objection.
- **Compliance slip:** if you're unsure whether a message is allowed, don't send it. Check ACMA's page or get advice.
