## Primary Action and Visual Identity (2026-10)

The primary action is **Tell us your problem**: a three-field problem box at the end of the
homepage story, reached from the header button on every page. **Book a Free Automation
Assessment** is the deeper step offered after a send. See `docs/conversion-strategy.md`.

The visual identity (everything except the logo) is a warm paper surface, ink text, a
logo-derived teal accent, Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans, and no people imagery. The
homepage tells its story in motion, and tokens and rules live in `docs/design-system.md`.

## Brand Position

Apprising Creatives helps Australian businesses eliminate repetitive work and modernize
operations with automation, AI, and custom business systems.

Apprising Corporation is the legal entity behind the brand (see `src/lib/constants.ts`'s
`SITE_LEGAL_NAME`).

We help businesses move beyond disconnected tools, repetitive work, and manual processes by
designing and building software systems that fit how they actually operate.

We also partner with agencies and consultancies that need reliable technical delivery capacity —
a real, separate part of the business, documented on its own page rather than the homepage.

## Core Message

### Primary

Your business runs on systems. We make them work smarter.

### Supporting

We help Australian businesses eliminate repetitive work, replace manual processes, and build
custom systems using automation, AI, and modern software.

The customer buys the business outcome. AI and software are the mechanism, not the pitch.

## Brand Promise

We do not start by recommending technology.

We start by understanding the business problem.

The goal is not to add AI everywhere.

The goal is to find the simplest and most effective technical solution — including, sometimes,
telling a prospect that no build is justified. See `docs/conversion-strategy.md`'s note on why
that possibility is part of the pitch, not a weakness of it.

## Audience

See `docs/target-audience.md` for the full ICP. Summary:

### Business Leaders (primary)

They care about:

* Saving time
* Reducing manual work
* Improving operations
* Connecting systems
* Scaling efficiently

They may not understand the technical implementation. The copy must therefore explain value
before technology.

### Agency Partners (secondary, own page)

They care about:

* Reliable delivery
* Technical expertise
* Communication
* Documentation
* Scalability
* Flexibility

They should see Apprising as an extension of their technical delivery capability. This
relationship is real and continues to run — it's just no longer the homepage's lead story.

## Voice

The brand voice is:

* Direct
* Intelligent
* Professional
* Human
* Calm
* Confident

The brand should never sound:

* Desperate
* Cheap
* Overly corporate
* Overly technical
* Like generic AI marketing

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. Business problem
2. Business impact
3. Solution
4. Technology
5. Implementation

Example:

Weak:

> We build AI-powered applications using Python and FastAPI.

Better:

> We automate repetitive business workflows and connect the systems your team relies on.

Then explain:

> When the right solution requires custom software or AI, we build it using modern, maintainable
> technology.

## Service Pillars

Three, not four — see `docs/conversion-strategy.md` and `docs/offers.md` for how the former
fourth pillar ("Managed Application Support") now lives as the **Business Systems Care Plan**, a
recurring offer rather than a homepage capability card.

### Business Automation

Eliminate repetitive manual work — workflow automation, repetitive administration, automated
notifications, document processing, data synchronization, CRM workflows, quotation workflows,
reporting automation, AI-assisted workflows.

### Custom Business Systems

Build software around the client's actual workflow — automated quotation systems, booking
systems, internal dashboards, customer portals, business management systems, custom CRM
functionality, API integrations, operational platforms.

### AI Implementation

Use AI where it creates measurable business value — AI assistants, AI agents, document
processing, enquiry handling, lead qualification, knowledge assistants, workflow intelligence,
AI-powered internal tools.

Cybersecurity remains an important supporting capability, not a primary commercial pillar — see
`docs/australian-market.md`'s Security section for exact messaging.

## Words to Use

* Business systems
* Operational efficiency
* Automation
* Integration
* Custom software
* Workflow
* Connected systems
* Business processes
* Practical AI
* Modernise / optimise / organisation (Australian spelling — see `docs/australian-market.md`)

## Words to Avoid

* Revolutionize your business
* Cutting-edge solutions
* Unlock the power of AI
* Leverage AI
* Transform everything / digital transformation
* Passionate about innovation
* Disruption / disruptive
* Game-changing / next-generation
* Synergy
* AI-powered everything

## Geographic Honesty

State plainly, not just avoid-by-omission — see `docs/australian-market.md` for exact copy and
placement:

> Built in the Philippines. Serving businesses internationally, including Australia.

## Do Not Invent

Clients, testimonials, metrics, revenue figures, ROI, case studies, compliance certifications,
ratings, or client counts. See `docs/trust-and-claims.md` for the live verification register and
`docs/case-studies.md` for case-study-specific rules. Demos and internal products must be
clearly labeled (MediFlow is the current example — internal product, no paying users, never
described otherwise).

## Agency Partnership Position

Core statement:

> Sell the solution. We'll help build it.

Supporting statement:

> Apprising Creatives works behind the scenes as an engineering partner for agencies and
> consultancies that need additional technical delivery capacity.

Lives entirely on `/for-agencies` as of this repositioning — see `docs/conversion-strategy.md`
for why it now runs as a separate funnel from the SME Assessment path rather than a homepage
section.
